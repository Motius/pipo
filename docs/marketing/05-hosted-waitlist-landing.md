## WHY HOSTED

**⏱ Always on, monitored.** The daemon's whole point is that it never sleeps. We keep it that way — health checks, restarts, snapshots. 3am watcher firings shouldn't depend on your server staying up.

**🔐 Your authority travels with you.** Approval cards, category gates and the audit trail come standard, whether pipo runs in your office or ours. Export any of it, any time.

**\\\ One assistant, every machine.** Your Windows and Linux machines pair as sidecars exactly like the self-hosted version — the brain lives in the cloud, the hands stay on your desks.

**🚀 Updates without maintenance days.** We ship updates and migrations; you get them. Nothing on your side breaks version drift.
