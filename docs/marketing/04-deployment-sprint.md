## QUALIFYING QUESTIONS FOR THE AUDIT CALL

1. What's the most repetitive computer task your team does weekly? (they will have 10; only recruit the one with a clear trigger)
2. Where does that data live, and who owns the apps involved?
3. Who says no when a system wants to send something to a customer? (if "nobody" — that IS the sales conversation)
4. Which machines are involved? (Windows/Linux OK; macOS = waitlist)
5. What would "this failed quietly" cost you? (sets the approval-priority design)
